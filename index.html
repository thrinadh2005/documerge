DocuMerge Studio does not operate any backend server, remote database, or cloud ingestion pipeline. All documents (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, Images) are parsed, merged, and rendered entirely inside your device's browser memory (RAM).

In adherence to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 and global data protection standards, this application enforces absolute Data Minimization. Because zero personal data is gathered, transmitted, or retained, your private records remain exclusively in your possession.
